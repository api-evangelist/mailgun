Gmail's new Verified Sender Program isn't the spam-filter bypass headlines are claiming; it just requires political senders to do what good deliverability already demands: authenticate their mail, keep volume predictable, and hold spam complaints under 0.3%. Verification proves who you are, but as the post argues, it can't make recipients want your email.
